The company

DoorDrop is a Tanzanian company for parcel delivery and cargo movement, headquartered in Dar es Salaam.

Who we are

DoorDrop is a Tanzanian company headquartered in Dar es Salaam. It organises parcel delivery and cargo movement across selected cities.

Work is organised around three service lines: motorcycle parcel delivery, TOYO cargo, and Kirikuu cargo. Operations are run from the head office in Dar es Salaam, with coverage in Mwanza, Arusha, Mtwara, and Morogoro.

This website publishes company information only. It is not a booking channel and does not accept delivery requests.
